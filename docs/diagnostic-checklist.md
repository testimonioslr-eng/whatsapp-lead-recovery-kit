# WhatsApp Funnel Diagnostic Checklist (10 minutes)

A self-audit you can run on a quiet afternoon. Answer yes/no for each
item. Score 1 point per "yes". If you score under 5, your WhatsApp funnel
is leaking leads you can recover.

## Section A - Inbox hygiene (max 3)

- [ ] I can tell, within 5 seconds of opening WhatsApp, whether a message
      is a new lead or an existing customer.
- [ ] I have a saved reply or template for the 3 most common first
      questions (price, availability, location).
- [ ] I have turned off notifications for groups and turned on custom
      notifications for unknown numbers so I see new leads first.

## Section B - Response time (max 3)

- [ ] I know what my average time-to-first-response was last week
      (not a guess, a real number).
- [ ] I have a goal for time-to-first-response (a number, in minutes).
- [ ] I have a backup (a person, an auto-reply) that handles the
      messages I receive when I am asleep, in a meeting, or driving.

## Section C - Attribution (max 2)

- [ ] When a new message arrives, I can name where it came from
      (TikTok ad, Instagram post, Google search, referral, walk-in).
- [ ] I write that source down before I reply, or it is captured
      automatically.

## Section D - Follow-up (max 2)

- [ ] I have a written follow-up sequence (at minimum: day 1 reply,
      day 3 nudge, day 7 last attempt) for leads who do not reply
      to my first message.
- [ ] I track which leads I sent a follow-up to and when.

## Score interpretation

- 9-10: your funnel is tight. Focus on growing volume, not fixing leaks.
- 6-8: you have a few leaks. Fix section B first, it usually pays back
       in days, not weeks.
- 3-5: significant leakage. Pick one item from B and one from D and
       fix them this week.
- 0-2: treat this as a "revenue emergency". Most of your WhatsApp
       inquiries are leaving to competitors who reply faster.
