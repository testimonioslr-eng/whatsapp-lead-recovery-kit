# Four WhatsApp recovery patterns, with tradeoffs

These are the four approaches we have seen small businesses use to
recover leads they would otherwise lose. None of them is "best". The
right one depends on your volume, your hours, and how much you are
willing to change your existing workflow.

## Pattern 1 - Saved replies and templates only

**What it is.** You pre-write 5-10 common answers in WhatsApp Business
and pick them from the template menu when a similar question arrives.

**Pros.** Free, takes a weekend to set up, no new tool.

**Cons.** Only helps if you already see and answer the message. Does
not help when you are asleep. Does not capture attribution.

**Best for.** Solo operators with fewer than 5 inquiries per day who
already answer promptly.

## Pattern 2 - Auto-reply for off-hours

**What it is.** WhatsApp Business "away message" set to fire when you
are not available. The message acknowledges receipt, sets expectations,
and tells the lead when to expect a human reply.

**Pros.** Free, takes 10 minutes to configure. Removes the worst
feeling a lead has - the "did they see my message?" feeling.

**Cons.** Still does not help if the lead sends the message during your
working hours and you take 3 hours to reply. Still does not capture
attribution.

**Best for.** Anyone who regularly receives messages while driving,
sleeping, or in long meetings.

## Pattern 3 - Routing + auto-assignment

**What it is.** A small backend (Google Forms + Sheets, or a dedicated
tool) that captures each new lead, tags it with the source, and routes
it to a specific person or a shared inbox. Some setups add a Slack or
email alert so the assigned person knows within a minute.

**Pros.** You start to know where your leads come from. You can give
each person a quota. You can measure response time per person.

**Cons.** Costs time to set up (4-10 hours for the first version).
Requires a discipline of checking the shared inbox.

**Best for.** Teams of 2-10 with a non-trivial volume (>20 leads/day)
and at least one person who is comfortable with Google Sheets or a
spreadsheet-driven workflow.

## Pattern 4 - End-to-end flow with attribution

**What it is.** The full pipeline: ad click -> landing page with UTM
-> WhatsApp handoff with the ad context attached -> tracking of the
response time and the outcome. The lead arrives at the salesperson
already tagged with what the lead saw, when, and from where.

**Pros.** Closest to "leak-proof" of the four. You know, for every
lead, what they saw and what you did. You can A/B test your landing
page. You can prove ROI of the ad spend.

**Cons.** Requires either building it (a weekend project for a
technical founder) or paying for a tool.

**Best for.** SMBs spending more than USD 500/month on ads who cannot
tell which ad is producing the closed customers.

## How to choose

If you are a solo operator, start with patterns 1 + 2. Most of the
gain is there. Patterns 3 and 4 start to make sense when you hire
your second person or when ad spend crosses USD 500/month.
